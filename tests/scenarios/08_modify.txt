# Reducing quantity at the same price keeps queue priority.
limit 1 buy 10 100
> accepted 1
limit 2 buy 10 100
> accepted 2
modify 1 4 100
> modified 1 4 100
market 3 sell 5
> accepted 3
> trade 3 1 sell 4 100
> trade 3 2 sell 1 100
book
> bid 100 9 (1)

# Increasing quantity loses priority: the order moves to the back of its level.
limit 4 buy 5 100
> accepted 4
modify 2 20 100
> modified 2 20 100
market 5 sell 6
> accepted 5
> trade 5 4 sell 5 100
> trade 5 2 sell 1 100
book
> bid 100 19 (1)

# A price change loses priority too, and the new price can trade right away.
limit 6 sell 8 103
> accepted 6
limit 7 buy 3 99
> accepted 7
modify 7 5 103
> modified 7 5 103
> trade 7 6 buy 5 103
book
> ask 103 3 (1)
> bid 100 19 (1)

# A modify that partly fills rests the rest at its new price.
modify 2 10 104
> modified 2 10 104
> trade 2 6 buy 3 103
book
> bid 104 7 (1)

# Rejected: a finished or unknown order, or zero quantity (use cancel for that).
modify 6 5 103
> rejected 6 unknown-order
modify 99 5 100
> rejected 99 unknown-order
modify 2 0 104
> rejected 2 zero-qty
book
> bid 104 7 (1)
