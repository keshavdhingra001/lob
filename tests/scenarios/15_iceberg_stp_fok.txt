# Icebergs with self-trade prevention and FOK (D86).

# STP covers the whole iceberg: the cancel reports shown and hidden quantity.
limit 1 sell 20 100 peak=5 g=1 stp=cn
> accepted 1
limit 2 sell 4 100
> accepted 2
limit 3 buy 10 100 g=1 stp=co
> accepted 3
> stp-cancelled 1 20
> trade 3 2 buy 4 100
book
> bid 100 6 (1)

# Where a same-group order blocks matching (cancel-newest or cancel-both), a FOK order
# can't count the hidden quantity ahead of it: each replenished slice goes behind the
# blocker. Here only 7 + 3 trade before matching reaches order 3.
config 1 1000000
limit 1 sell 7 100
> accepted 1
limit 2 sell 30 100 peak=3
> accepted 2
limit 3 sell 5 100 g=1 stp=cn
> accepted 3
limit 4 buy 11 100 fok g=1 stp=cn
> accepted 4
> cancelled 4 11
limit 5 buy 10 100 fok g=1 stp=cn
> accepted 5
> trade 5 1 buy 7 100
> trade 5 2 buy 3 100
> replenished 2 3
book
> ask 100 8 (2)
