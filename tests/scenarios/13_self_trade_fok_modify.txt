# FOK with self-trade prevention (D71): same-group quantity never fills an order.
limit 1 sell 5 100 g=1 stp=cn
> accepted 1
limit 2 sell 5 100
> accepted 2
limit 3 sell 5 101
> accepted 3

# Cancel oldest skips the group's 5: only 10 is fillable, so 11 is killed, and a
# killed FOK cancels nothing.
limit 4 buy 11 101 fok g=1 stp=co
> accepted 4
> cancelled 4 11
book
> ask 101 5 (1)
> ask 100 10 (2)

# 10 fills, cancelling the group's order on the way.
limit 5 buy 10 101 fok g=1 stp=co
> accepted 5
> stp-cancelled 1 5
> trade 5 2 buy 5 100
> trade 5 3 buy 5 101
book
> empty

# Cancel newest stops at the first same-group order, so only the 5 ahead of it counts.
limit 6 sell 5 100
> accepted 6
limit 7 sell 5 100 g=1 stp=cn
> accepted 7
limit 8 sell 5 100
> accepted 8
limit 9 buy 6 100 fok g=1 stp=cn
> accepted 9
> cancelled 9 6
limit 10 buy 5 100 fok g=1 stp=cb
> accepted 10
> trade 10 6 buy 5 100
book
> ask 100 10 (2)

# A modify that crosses makes the order a taker, with its own action (D69).
limit 11 buy 5 99 g=1 stp=cb
> accepted 11
modify 11 5 100
> modified 11 5 100
> stp-cancelled 7 5
> stp-cancelled 11 5
book
> ask 100 5 (1)

# Post-only is unchanged: crossing its own group is still a reject.
limit 12 sell 1 99 g=3 stp=co
> accepted 12
limit 13 buy 1 99 post g=3 stp=co
> rejected 13 would-cross
