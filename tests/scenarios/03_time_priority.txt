# At one price, the oldest order fills first (FIFO).
limit 1 buy 10 10000
> accepted 1
limit 2 buy 10 10000
> accepted 2
limit 3 buy 10 10000
> accepted 3
market 4 sell 15
> accepted 4
> trade 4 1 sell 10 10000
> trade 4 2 sell 5 10000
book
> bid 10000 15 (2)

# A partial fill keeps its place: order 2 is still ahead of order 3.
market 5 sell 6
> accepted 5
> trade 5 2 sell 5 10000
> trade 5 3 sell 1 10000
book
> bid 10000 9 (1)

# A new order at the same price queues behind the ones already there.
limit 6 buy 4 10000
> accepted 6
market 7 sell 10
> accepted 7
> trade 7 3 sell 9 10000
> trade 7 6 sell 1 10000
book
> bid 10000 3 (1)
