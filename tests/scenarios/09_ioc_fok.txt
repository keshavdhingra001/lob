# IOC fills what crosses now and cancels the rest. It never rests.
limit 3 buy 8 100 ioc
> accepted 3
> trade 3 1 buy 5 100
> cancelled 3 3
book
> ask 101 5 (1)

# FOK: only 5 crosses at or below 101 (the 5 at 102 is beyond the limit), so it's
# killed whole and nothing trades.
limit 4 sell 5 102
> accepted 4
limit 5 buy 6 101 fok
> accepted 5
> cancelled 5 6
book
> ask 102 5 (1)
> ask 101 5 (1)

# With a higher limit there's enough: it fills completely, across levels.
limit 6 buy 7 102 fok
> accepted 6
> trade 6 2 buy 5 101
> trade 6 4 buy 2 102
book
> ask 102 3 (1)

# An IOC that crosses nothing is cancelled whole.
limit 7 sell 4 105 ioc
> accepted 7
> cancelled 7 4
book
> ask 102 3 (1)
