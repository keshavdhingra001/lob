# Better prices fill first, and every fill trades at the maker's price.
limit 1 sell 10 10300
> accepted 1
limit 2 sell 10 10100
> accepted 2
limit 3 sell 10 10200
> accepted 3

# Buying up to 10250 sweeps 10100, then 10200, and stops before 10300.
# The taker pays 10100 and 10200, not its limit (price improvement), and rests the rest.
limit 4 buy 25 10250
> accepted 4
> trade 4 2 buy 10 10100
> trade 4 3 buy 10 10200
book
> ask 10300 10 (1)
> bid 10250 5 (1)

# The mirror image: a sell sweeps the highest bids first.
limit 5 buy 10 10000
> accepted 5
limit 6 buy 10 9900
> accepted 6
limit 7 sell 20 9950
> accepted 7
> trade 7 4 sell 5 10250
> trade 7 5 sell 10 10000
book
> ask 10300 10 (1)
> ask 9950 5 (1)
> bid 9900 10 (1)
