# Tick size 5, max quantity 100.
config 5 100
limit 1 buy 10 1003
> rejected 1 bad-tick
limit 1 buy 101 1000
> rejected 1 qty-too-large
market 1 sell 101
> rejected 1 qty-too-large
limit 1 buy 100 1000
> accepted 1
limit 2 sell 10 1005
> accepted 2

# A modify is validated like a new order, and a rejected one changes nothing.
modify 2 10 1007
> rejected 2 bad-tick
modify 2 101 1005
> rejected 2 qty-too-large
book
> ask 1005 10 (1)
> bid 1000 100 (1)

# Negative prices on the grid are fine.
limit 3 sell 5 -15
> accepted 3
> trade 3 1 sell 5 1000

# A new config starts over with an empty book.
config 1 1000000
book
> empty
limit 1 buy 1 1003
> accepted 1
