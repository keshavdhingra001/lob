# A post-only order that would trade is rejected, and its id stays free.
limit 2 buy 5 101 post
> rejected 2 would-cross
# One tick lower it adds liquidity, so it rests.
limit 2 buy 5 100 post
> accepted 2
book
> ask 101 5 (1)
> bid 100 5 (1)

# A post-only order keeps its protection: a modify that would cross is rejected,
# and the order stays exactly as it was.
modify 2 5 101
> rejected 2 would-cross
book
> ask 101 5 (1)
> bid 100 5 (1)

# A plain limit order can be modified across the spread, and it trades.
limit 3 buy 2 99
> accepted 3
modify 3 2 101
> modified 3 2 101
> trade 3 1 buy 2 101
book
> ask 101 3 (1)
> bid 100 5 (1)

# Reducing a post-only order in place is fine.
modify 2 1 100
> modified 2 1 100
book
> ask 101 3 (1)
> bid 100 1 (1)

# It's still post-only after a modify that moved it.
modify 2 3 99
> modified 2 3 99
modify 2 3 101
> rejected 2 would-cross
book
> ask 101 3 (1)
> bid 99 3 (1)
