# Iceberg orders (D83-D85, D88). Only the peak shows. When the shown slice is used up,
# the next one shows at the back of its level, and matching may reach it again.
# `book` prints shown quantity only, as market data does.

# Entry: peak < qty, on an order that can rest (GTC or post-only). (peak=0 doesn't parse.)
limit 1 sell 10 100 peak=10
> rejected 1 bad-peak
limit 1 sell 10 100 ioc peak=5
> rejected 1 bad-peak
limit 1 sell 10 100 fok peak=5
> rejected 1 bad-peak
limit 1 sell 0 100 peak=5
> rejected 1 zero-qty

# 30 to sell, 10 at a time. Order 2 arrives later but sits behind only the first slice.
limit 1 sell 30 100 peak=10
> accepted 1
limit 2 sell 5 100
> accepted 2
book
> ask 100 15 (2)

# The first slice trades, the next one goes behind order 2 (D84).
limit 3 buy 12 100
> accepted 3
> trade 3 1 buy 10 100
> replenished 1 10
> trade 3 2 buy 2 100
book
> ask 100 13 (2)

# One taker can take several slices in one sweep (D85).
market 4 buy 30
> accepted 4
> trade 4 2 buy 3 100
> trade 4 1 buy 10 100
> replenished 1 10
> trade 4 1 buy 10 100
> cancelled 4 7
book
> empty

# The last slice is whatever is left.
limit 5 buy 25 99 peak=10
> accepted 5
book
> bid 99 10 (1)
market 6 sell 12
> accepted 6
> trade 6 5 sell 10 99
> replenished 5 10
> trade 6 5 sell 2 99
book
> bid 99 8 (1)
market 7 sell 13
> accepted 7
> trade 7 5 sell 8 99
> replenished 5 5
> trade 7 5 sell 5 99
book
> empty

# A cancel reports shown and hidden quantity together.
limit 8 buy 25 99 peak=10
> accepted 8
cancel 8
> cancelled 8 25

# An incoming iceberg trades its whole size, then rests what's left behind its peak.
limit 9 sell 4 100
> accepted 9
limit 10 sell 4 101
> accepted 10
limit 11 buy 20 101 peak=5
> accepted 11
> trade 11 9 buy 4 100
> trade 11 10 buy 4 101
book
> bid 101 5 (1)

# Post-only icebergs are allowed.
limit 12 sell 10 101 post peak=2
> rejected 12 would-cross
limit 12 sell 10 102 post peak=2
> accepted 12
book
> ask 102 2 (1)
> bid 101 5 (1)

# Modify sets the whole open quantity (D88).
config 1 1000000
limit 1 buy 20 100 peak=5
> accepted 1
limit 2 buy 3 100
> accepted 2
book
> bid 100 8 (2)

# A decrease comes out of the hidden quantity first, and keeps the order's place.
modify 1 12 100
> modified 1 12 100
book
> bid 100 8 (2)
modify 1 3 100
> modified 1 3 100
book
> bid 100 6 (2)

# An increase loses priority: the order rests again at the back, showing its peak.
modify 1 20 100
> modified 1 20 100
book
> bid 100 8 (2)
market 3 sell 6
> accepted 3
> trade 3 2 sell 3 100
> trade 3 1 sell 3 100
book
> bid 100 2 (1)

# So does a new price, and the order can trade on the way with its whole size.
limit 4 sell 4 101
> accepted 4
modify 1 17 101
> modified 1 17 101
> trade 1 4 buy 4 101
book
> bid 101 5 (1)

# Below the peak, everything shows.
modify 1 3 102
> modified 1 3 102
book
> bid 102 3 (1)

# A FOK order counts hidden quantity: matching will reach all of it (D86).
config 1 1000000
limit 1 sell 20 100 peak=5
> accepted 1
limit 2 buy 21 100 fok
> accepted 2
> cancelled 2 21
limit 3 buy 20 100 fok
> accepted 3
> trade 3 1 buy 5 100
> replenished 1 5
> trade 3 1 buy 5 100
> replenished 1 5
> trade 3 1 buy 5 100
> replenished 1 5
> trade 3 1 buy 5 100
book
> empty
