# Cancel from the middle of a queue; the orders around it keep their order.
cancel 2
> cancelled 2 20
# Cancelling the only order at a price removes the level.
cancel 4
> cancelled 4 5

> bid 10000 40 (2)

> trade 5 3 sell 5 10000

# Cancelling twice, or an id that never existed, is rejected.
cancel 2
> rejected 2 unknown-order
cancel 99
> rejected 99 unknown-order

# A partly filled order cancels with only what's left.
cancel 3
> cancelled 3 25

# A fully filled order can't be cancelled.

> accepted 6
