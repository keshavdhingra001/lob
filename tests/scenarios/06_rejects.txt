# Zero quantity is rejected and changes nothing.
limit 1 buy 0 10000
> rejected 1 zero-qty
market 2 sell 0
> rejected 2 zero-qty
book
> empty

# A rejected order doesn't use up its id.
limit 1 buy 10 10000
> accepted 1

# Reusing an id is rejected while the order rests...
limit 1 sell 5 10100
> rejected 1 duplicate-id
# ...after it's cancelled...
cancel 1
> cancelled 1 10
limit 1 buy 10 10000
> rejected 1 duplicate-id
# ...and after a market order is done.
market 2 buy 5
> accepted 2
> cancelled 2 5
market 2 buy 5
> rejected 2 duplicate-id
book
> empty
