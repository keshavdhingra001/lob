# Zero quantity is rejected and changes nothing.
limit 1 buy 0 10000
> rejected 1 zero-qty
market 2 sell 0
> rejected 2 zero-qty
book
> empty

# A rejected order doesn't use up its id.
limit 1 buy 10 10000
> accepted 1

# Ids must increase (D30), so reusing an id is rejected while the order rests...
limit 1 sell 5 10100
> rejected 1 id-not-increasing
# ...after it's cancelled...
cancel 1
> cancelled 1 10
limit 1 buy 10 10000
> rejected 1 id-not-increasing
# ...and after a market order is done.
market 2 buy 5
> accepted 2
> cancelled 2 5
market 2 buy 5
> rejected 2 id-not-increasing
book
> empty

# Gaps are fine: an id only has to beat the highest one accepted so far.
limit 10 buy 1 10000
> accepted 10
# A lower id is rejected even though it was never used.
limit 5 buy 1 10000
> rejected 5 id-not-increasing
# Rejected orders don't raise the bar: 12 is refused (bad qty), so 11 is still fine.
limit 12 buy 0 10000
> rejected 12 zero-qty
limit 11 buy 1 10000
> accepted 11
book
> bid 10000 2 (2)
