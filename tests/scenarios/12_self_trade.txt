# Self-trade prevention (D67-D70). Orders in the same STP group never trade with each
# other; the incoming order's action decides what happens instead.
limit 1 sell 5 100 g=1 stp=cn
> accepted 1
limit 2 sell 5 100
> accepted 2
limit 3 sell 5 101 g=1 stp=cn
> accepted 3

# Cancel oldest: each same-group resting order is cancelled and matching goes on.
# What's left rests as usual.
limit 4 buy 12 101 g=1 stp=co
> accepted 4
> stp-cancelled 1 5
> trade 4 2 buy 5 100
> stp-cancelled 3 5
book
> bid 101 7 (1)
cancel 4
> cancelled 4 7

# Cancel newest: the incoming order's remainder is cancelled, even though it's GTC.
# Fills before that stand, and the resting order keeps its place in the queue.
limit 5 sell 5 100
> accepted 5
limit 6 sell 5 100 g=1 stp=co
> accepted 6
limit 7 sell 5 100
> accepted 7
limit 8 buy 12 100 g=1 stp=cn
> accepted 8
> trade 8 5 buy 5 100
> stp-cancelled 8 7
book
> ask 100 10 (2)

# Cancel both: the resting order's cancel comes first.
limit 9 buy 3 100 g=1 stp=cb
> accepted 9
> stp-cancelled 6 5
> stp-cancelled 9 3
book
> ask 100 5 (1)

# Another group trades normally.
limit 10 buy 2 100 g=2 stp=cn
> accepted 10
> trade 10 7 buy 2 100

# So does an ungrouped order, against grouped ones.
limit 11 sell 4 100 g=1 stp=cb
> accepted 11
market 12 buy 5
> accepted 12
> trade 12 7 buy 3 100
> trade 12 11 buy 2 100
book
> ask 100 2 (1)

# A market order carries a group too. Nothing else crosses, so the rest is cancelled
# as for any market order.
market 13 buy 5 g=1 stp=co
> accepted 13
> stp-cancelled 11 2
> cancelled 13 5
book
> empty
